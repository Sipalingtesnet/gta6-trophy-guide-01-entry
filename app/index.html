    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). The story runs 5 chapters and takes roughly 60–80 hours. There are no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals and no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots, 1 autosave). Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so leave them alone until the platinum is done.

    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as possibly missable. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks to the balcony at Jason's house. Follow her, accept, and let her show you how. That session raises the meditation counter but is not listed as a meditation spot, and it is still unconfirmed whether it counts toward the trophy — do it anyway, because it is gone after Chapter 1. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 ~60–80 hours

      Just finish the story and enjoy it. The 100% checklist is not visible until after the story, but any progress you make during it already counts. Scan new vehicles and animals as you go (WAiNK app for vehicles, binoculars for animals), meditate at the spots you pass, and do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation above. Optional missions with black icons and pink letters can disappear, but that never blocks a trophy: there are far more than the 100 the checklist wants.

    2. Stage 2 — 100% Completion Checklist & challenges ~100 hours

      After the credits, open the checklist (Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion). It is lenient — closer to 60% of true completion: most side quests are not needed, collectibles are only partly needed, and you need any 70 of the 80 challenges. The only rows that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests. This stage earns most of the remaining trophies on its own.

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

      Finish the stranger strands, the five still-unconfirmed secret trophies (check their guides — they are marked unconfirmed until a published guide pins them down), and whatever is left. The platinum pops with the last trophy.

    The 100% checklist, at a glance

    • Any 100 optional missions (there are 200–300+).
    • Scan any 200 of 277 base-model vehicles; study any 100 of 255 animals with binoculars.
    • 300 of 397 stunts; all shooting ranges, all drone races, all 39 gang operations, all 8 bank vaults, all 15 of Bret's target decks.
    • Any 70 of 80 challenges; 15 of 18 meditations.
    • All collectible rows are partial — only the rows above need everything.

    About this guide

    GTA VI released on 19 November 2026, the day this guide was written. Its guides are built from the official trophy descriptions plus published launch guides — chiefly PowerPyx, with Exophase and PSNProfiles for the names, descriptions, grades and counts. Where a step is not yet confirmed by any published guide, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing. Steps may shift as more of the game is mapped after launch; the five trophies PowerPyx still lists as unknown are marked unconfirmed in their guides.

    How the tracker works

    • Tick a trophy and it stays ticked: progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.
    • Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import reads one back. The file only holds trophy names and the date — nothing else.
    • Filters cover story, missable, collectibles, activities, side quests, crime & robberies, secret trophies and platinum/100%. The list has no online trophies — all 55 are offline single-player, so the Online filter says so instead of showing an empty list.
    • Hide story spoilers replaces the names and descriptions of story trophies you have not earned yet, and their guides, until you earn them.
